# Free Features & Tab Insights

## 1. Horoscope
Track your day-to-day emotional currents through your Moon sign (Rashi), not generic sun sign predictions. The Moon governs the subconscious, inner serenity, and daily relational rhythms. Discover how current lunar transits trigger specific houses in your natal chart, revealing auspicious windows for conversation, reflection, and decision-making.

## 2. Kundli
Your Janam Kundli is your cosmic birth blueprint cast at the exact minute and coordinates of your arrival on earth. Explore the 12 Bhavas (houses), planetary dignities, Shadbala strengths, and key divisional charts including the Navamsa (D9) and Dasamsa (D10). Understand the foundational yogas and karmic patterns shaping your life trajectory.

## 3. Partner
Examine the 7th house of sacred partnership, Venus, Jupiter, and the intricate dynamics of the Navamsa (D9) chart. Through authentic Ashtakoota Guna Milan and planetary compatibility analysis, decode emotional harmony, physical attraction, shared values, and potential karmic challenges between two souls before marital commitment.

## 4. Dasha
Time is cyclical in Parashari Jyotish. Journey through the Vimshottari Dasha system—the 120-year planetary cycle of Mahadashas, Antardashas, and Pratyantardashas. Unveil which celestial forces are active right now, when major turning points in career and marriage unfold, and when quiet periods of preparation are warranted.

## 5. Past events
Validate the precision of classical Parashari astrology through your personal history. Cross-reference major life milestones—graduations, relocations, career breakthroughs, and past relationships—against the planetary periods and Gochara (transits) that presided over them, building trust in your upcoming forecast.
